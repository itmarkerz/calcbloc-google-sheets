Regional tables hold only the regional figures; the national table is a separate insert.

Adds a new sheet with every band, threshold and rate, each with its official source link and the date it was last checked. The credit "Data by CalcBloc (calcbloc.com/data)" and a link to the calcbloc.com page are included: keep them when you share the figures.

Without a key the API allows 50 requests a day per internet address, and Google's servers share addresses, so formulas can hit that limit. A free key gives 5,000 a day. The key is stored in your Google account's settings for this add-on and sent only to calcbloc.com.

Used when a formula leaves the country blank, e.g. =CALCBLOC_NET(, A2). Saved with this spreadsheet.

=CALCBLOC_NET(country, gross, [region], [year], [options])
Annual take-home pay. =CALCBLOC_NET("us", 60000, "ca")
=CALCBLOC_TAX(country, gross, [region], [year], [options])
Annual income tax (no social security). =CALCBLOC_TAX("canada", 85000, "on")
=CALCBLOC_EMPLOYER_COST(country, salary, [region], [year], [options])
Salary plus employer on-costs. =CALCBLOC_EMPLOYER_COST("uk", 40000)
=CALCBLOC_RATE(country, section, label, [year], [region])
One figure from the tax tables. =CALCBLOC_RATE("norway", "general-income", "Individuals")
=CALCBLOC_BAND_TAX(country, taxable_income, [section], [year], [region])
Tax from a band schedule, before allowances and credits. =CALCBLOC_BAND_TAX("austria", 40000)
=CALCBLOC_COUNTRIES()
Every country with its tax year and what can be calculated.

Pass a range (e.g. A2:A50) as the salary to fill a column with one formula. Results are cached for up to 6 hours. Calculations use the current tax year; earlier years are available as tables.

Figures are estimates for planning, not tax advice. Sources and notes: calcbloc.com/data · API: calcbloc.com/developers
